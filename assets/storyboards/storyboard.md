# Storyboard — SC03 "Platform Standoff"

Four frames, one per setup in `assets/shot-list/shot_list.csv`. Each `.svg` in this
folder is a simple labeled placeholder frame (not concept art) — composition and
action are captured in the description text below, which is what grounds the
Continuity agent's "intended vs captured" check.

## Frame 1 — Setup 1 (Master, wide, 24mm, dolly push)

`setup1.svg`

Wide shot, camera at platform level, both actors visible in frame with Mara foreground-
left near the platform edge and Deacon entering frame-right six feet away. The torn
missing-person poster is visible on the tiled wall between them. As the beat progresses
the camera dollies forward toward Mara. The train enters from the tunnel mouth background
and passes through frame in a wall of light at the pyro/lighting cue point. Deacon has
closed half the distance by the time the train clears.

## Frame 2 — Setup 2 (Single on Mara, close, 50mm, handheld push)

`setup2.svg`

Close single on Mara, handheld, slow push in. Her expression is guarded, controlled.
The train's headlight sweep crosses her face once as it passes (lighting cue). She does
not flinch. Coverage of her "I disappeared. That's what happened to me" line and the
listening beat afterward.

## Frame 3 — Setup 3 (Reverse single on Deacon, close, 50mm, static)

`setup3.svg`

Static close single on Deacon, reverse of setup 2's eyeline. Calm through the whole
exchange; on his last line ("Then disappear again...") he is closer to camera than his
master-shot blocking implied, matching his approach in setup 1. Button of the scene:
the almost-smile on "You'd hate it."

## Frame 4 — Setup 4 (Clean plate, wide, 24mm, static)

`setup4.svg`

Same wide framing as setup 1's opening position, wall only, no actors, no train — a
clean background plate for VFX to paint out the missing-person poster if a later cut
needs Mara's backstory reveal removed from this scene.
