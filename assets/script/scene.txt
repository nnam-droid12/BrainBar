SC03 — INT. SUBWAY PLATFORM — NIGHT

A late-night subway platform, empty except for flickering fluorescent light and the
distant rumble of an approaching train. MARA VOSS (30s, guarded, still in her transit-
authority jacket) stands near the platform edge, facing DEACON REYES (40s, calm,
dangerous) six feet away.

A poster for a missing person — Mara's face, ten years younger — hangs torn on the
tiled wall behind them.

                    DEACON
          You always did like the last car. Furthest
          from the cameras.

                    MARA
          I stopped caring about the cameras when you
          started showing up in my dreams.

He takes one step closer. Wind begins to stir the platform trash — the train is close.

                    DEACON
          I'm not here to relitigate. I'm here because
          the Bureau wants to know the same thing I do.

                    MARA
          What happened to the money?

                    DEACON
          What happened to *you*.

The train's headlight sweeps the tunnel mouth behind Deacon. Mara doesn't flinch.

                    MARA
          I disappeared. That's what happened to me.

The train roars through the platform — does not stop — a wall of light and noise. In
the strobe of passing windows, Deacon has closed half the distance. When the train
clears, he's close enough to touch her.

                    DEACON
                (quiet, under the fading roar)
          Then disappear again. Tonight. And this time
          I go with you.

Mara studies him. Somewhere behind them, a station announcement crackles, unintelligible.

                    MARA
          You'd hate it. No cameras to perform for.

A beat. Then, for the first time, Deacon almost smiles.
